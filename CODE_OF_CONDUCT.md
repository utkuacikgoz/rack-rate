# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can send a correction, a
measurement, or a fix without friction. That means keeping discussion focused
on the data and the code, not on the people involved.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Sticking to what a number is, where it came from, and whether it is right
- Disagreeing with a figure by adding evidence, not by asserting louder
- Assuming good faith on incomplete submissions. A partial measurement is
  still useful.

Examples of behaviour that does not help:

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissing someone instead of their evidence
- Harassment in any form, public or private
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening an issue tagged `conduct`, or by
contacting the maintainers directly if the report itself should not be
public. Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove comments, close
threads, or ban contributors whose conduct violates this policy.

## Scope

This applies within the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any
other space where someone is representing the project.
