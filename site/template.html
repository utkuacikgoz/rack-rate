Rack Rate

API list pricing is the rack rate.
Nobody pays it.

A hotel's rack rate is the price on the back of the door. DeepSWE scores 21 coding models on 113 original engineering tasks and prices every one at API list rates. This page rejoins those scores to what a coding subscription actually charges, and cites every number.

How long a quota lasts

A price per task hides the ceiling. This is how long all 113 tasks would take on each route, limited by whichever cap bites first: the monthly quota or the rolling five hour window.

The method

cost_per_task = price_per_month / tasks_per_month. What counts as a task depends on which unit the vendor publishes its quota in.

The cross check

Two vendors publish their measured quota twice, once in dollars and once in tokens. Counting both ways shares no input, since tokens per task come from a separate agent run. A stable gap between the two answers points at a systematic cause rather than noise.
